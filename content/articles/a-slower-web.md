---
title: A slower web
date: 2026-09-28
summary: Pages that arrive all at once, say one thing, and let you leave without a chase.
---

I want pages that load like a room you have already been in. No carousel. No trap. A thing to read, a way onward, and the sense that the person who made it is still somewhere nearby.

Speed still matters. A slow page is not the same as a slower web. The slowness I mean is editorial: fewer doors, longer halls, words that were allowed to sit before they were published.

This journal is that experiment. One film. A handful of sheets. You write a file, you redeploy, another page joins the wind.
