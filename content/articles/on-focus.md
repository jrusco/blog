---
title: On focus
date: 2026-10-20
summary: A narrow hour, a closed door, and the work that only shows up when the rest is quiet.
---

Focus is not a mood I wait for. It is a room I make.

I close the extra tabs, put the phone in another room, and give the first block a single sentence: what would make this hour finished. Not impressive. Finished. The sentence is allowed to be small.

The field helps. Not the metaphor — the actual one, when I can get there. Wind does not ask for a status update. The work either moved or it did not, and the light changes either way.
