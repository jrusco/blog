---
title: Field notes
date: 2026-09-02
summary: What I keep when a day was mostly weather, errands, and one useful thought.
---

Not every day produces an essay. Some days produce a line I do not want to lose: a bug that was a naming problem, a walk that fixed a paragraph, a tool I will forget if I do not write down why it helped.

So the field notes stay short on purpose. Date, what happened, what I would repeat. If it grows into something else, it gets its own sheet. If it does not, it was still worth catching before the wind took it.

The bar is low. The file still has to exist.
