---
title: Quiet tools
date: 2026-08-14
summary: Software that sits still until you need it, then does one job without narrating itself.
---

The tools I stay with are the quiet ones. A editor that opens on the last file. A notebook that does not congratulate me. A deploy that finishes and gets out of the way.

I notice the loud ones faster, and trust them less. They explain themselves while I am trying to think. Quiet tools leave the sentence on the page and the rest of the room intact.

That is the kind of thing I want this place to be. You arrive, the night is already there, and the writing comes past when you are ready to take a page.
