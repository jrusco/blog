---
title: Night shift
date: 2026-10-12
summary: Notes from the hours after the inbox gives up, when the screen is the only warm thing in the grass.
---

The late hours are honest. Nobody is performing in them. The laptop is just a lamp, and the sentence on it is either true or it is not.

I keep a short list for those hours: one bug, one paragraph, one thing I have been avoiding because it needs quiet rather than cleverness. If I finish it, I stop. Heroics at midnight become sludge in the morning.

The stars do not care which one I picked. That is the nicest part.
